Policy

Welcome to our Policy page. This document outlines the guidelines and practices that govern the use of our website and services.

Introduction​

This Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect the information of our users. It applies to everyone who visits our website or uses our services.

Scope​

This Policy covers:

  • Data collection and usage.
  • User privacy and security.
  • Use of cookies and tracking technologies.
  • Our commitment to transparency and compliance with applicable laws.

Data Collection​

We collect information when you interact with our website, such as:

  • Information you provide directly via forms.
  • Data collected automatically through cookies and similar technologies.

Data Usage​

The information we collect is used to:

  • Enhance and personalize your experience.
  • Improve our services.
  • Communicate updates and relevant information to you.

Cookies and Tracking​

We use cookies and other tracking technologies to:

  • Analyze website traffic.
  • Customize user experiences.
  • Support our marketing efforts.

You can manage your cookie preferences through your browser settings.

Security​

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your information from unauthorized access, disclosure, or alteration. However, please note that no method of transmission over the Internet is completely secure.

Changes to This Policy​

We may update this Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page, and your continued use of our services after those changes signifies your acceptance of the new terms.

Contact Information​

If you have any questions or concerns about this Policy, please contact us at [https://besanh.github.io].
